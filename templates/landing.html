Fair questions

Straight answers, the same way we write proposals.

Does the AI make up the savings numbers? No — and that's the point. All money math runs in Python from assumptions you and the client can see and edit. The AI only restates the client's goals and explains the results in plain language. Every figure in its writing traces back to a stored field.
Do you guarantee savings or results? Never. Every figure is labelled an estimate with its assumptions shown. If a value can't be honestly quantified — like "consistent posting" — it's presented as a benefit, not a number.
I'm a solo VA, not an agency. Does this still fit? It's built for you first. In solo mode, proposals present your own verified skills and rates, and the matching engine checks whether you should take a deal — with honest gaps and what to do about them.
Who owns my client data? You do. Your clients, notes, and proposals are yours, exportable. We keep daily backups, and we never train on your data.
Can I cancel anytime? Yes. Monthly plans cancel in a click; your data stays exportable for 90 days.
