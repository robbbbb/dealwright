Fair questions

Straight answers, the same way we write proposals.

Does the AI make up the savings numbers?

No. All money math runs in code from assumptions you and the client can see and edit. The AI explains the results; every figure traces back to a stored field.

Do you guarantee savings or results?

Never. Every figure is labelled as an estimate with its assumptions shown.

I’m a solo VA, not an agency. Does this still fit?

Yes. Solo mode presents your own verified skills and rates while checking your fit and identifying honest gaps.

Who owns my client data?

You do. Your clients, notes, and proposals remain yours and are exportable.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.
