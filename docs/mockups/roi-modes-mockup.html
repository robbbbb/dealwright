Dynamic ROI — one engine, four honest modes

Social media VAs, automation builds, private-audience work: the estimate isn't one formula. Each catalog service declares what it may claim; the session decides what can actually be computed. The engine picks the mode — and when nothing can be computed, it says so instead of inventing numbers.

A claim classifier, not a number. It says which levers a service may ever use in an estimate — seeded per catalog row, provider-editable. Your price-only catalog stays price-only: no percentages, no dollar figures live here.

Mode selection (per workflow × service — computed, never guessed)

automation allowed + hours & cost captured → AUTOMATION math
delegation allowed + performed-by ≠ nobody → DELEGATION math
revenue allowed + lead data captured → REVENUE math

Example: Social media VA × "posting & DMs" (Client, 6h/wk) → delegation mode. Private podcast × "nobody today" → qualitative only.

Follow-up questions (would unlock computation)

Q. Who will own podcast production after this — you, a team member, or the VA?
Q. Roughly how many hours per episode do you spend when you do publish?
Answers re-run mode selection on save. Until then, the estimate stays honestly empty of numbers.

6The honesty rules (engine guardrails)

  1. One mode per estimate line. Automation and delegation are never blended into one number — no double-counted claims. Extra efficiency (e.g., a VA using schedulers) is narrative, not math.
  2. Nobody does it today → qualitative. Hours that don't exist can't be reclaimed. No invented baselines.
  3. Bands are selected, never invented. Benchmark values ship as versioned data with sources; every figure on screen traces to a stored assumption (session, benchmark band, or provider override — each labelled).
  4. The LLM never does math. It extracts facts and writes narrative; every peso comes from this deterministic engine.
  5. No FX, ever. Currency passes through as declared.
  6. Everything is an estimate — labelled as such, recomputed on save, assumptions shown everywhere.
