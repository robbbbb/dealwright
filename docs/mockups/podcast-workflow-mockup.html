🎙️ Podcast Episode Announcer

Upload your sermon audio to a Google Drive folder — your churchmates automatically get an email with a Listen button. Here's what it will look like.

1 · The workflow (as it will appear in n8n)

Runs on your own n8n instance. Checks your Drive folder every 15 minutes — you do nothing after you hit upload.

📁 Google Drive Trigger
📁
New File in Podcast Folder
Watches your "Church Podcast" folder · every 15 min
🔎 Filter
🔊
Is Audio File?
Only continues for .mp3 · .m4a · .wav · .aac · .ogg
✉️ Gmail
✉️
Send Announcement Email
Sends the episode email to your member list
💡 Later upgrade: want members to subscribe in Spotify / Apple Podcasts? The same audio can go to free hosting (Spotify for Creators), and this workflow can share that link instead — so members get new episodes in their podcast app automatically.

2 · What happens when you upload

Example episode: Faith Over Fear — Sunday Message

🎵
Faith_Over_Fear_Sunday_Message.mp3
Uploaded to Google Drive → "Church Podcast" folder · 38.2 MB
⬇  within 15 minutes, automatically  ⬇
  1. The workflow detects the new file in the folder.
  2. The filter checks it's an audio file (.mp3, .m4a, .wav…) — stray documents or images are ignored.
  3. The filename becomes the episode title: Faith Over Fear — Sunday Message (extension removed).
  4. An announcement email goes out to your churchmates — preview below ⬇
📣 Because the Drive folder is shared as "Anyone with the link → Viewer", members just tap the button and listen — no Google account, no app install needed.
{
  "name": "Faith_Over_Fear_Sunday_Message.mp3",   // → becomes the title
  "webViewLink": "https://drive.google.com/file/d/1aB2…/view",  // → the Listen button
  "mimeType": "audio/mpeg"
}

4 · When you're ready to make it real (~10 min, one time)

  1. Create the "Church Podcast" folder in Google Drive and share it Anyone with the link → Viewer.
  2. In n8n: Workflows → Import from File → pick podcast-episode-announcer.n8n.json.
  3. Open the Drive trigger node → connect your Google account → select the folder.
  4. Open the Gmail node → connect your Gmail → put your members' emails in To (or one free Google Group address so you manage the list in one place).
  5. Toggle the workflow Active. Done — just upload and it sends.
The importable workflow file is already prepared and sitting next to this mockup: podcast-episode-announcer.n8n.json. Nothing is live until you import, connect credentials, and activate.
